Usage & Fair Use

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of AI-handled call minutes. If you exceed your included minutes, calls continue uninterrupted at the listed overage rate.

SMS notifications are included under fair use for service-related messages only (e.g. booking confirmations, lead notifications, and system alerts). Bulk or marketing SMS is not included.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will calls ever stop if I hit my limit?
No. Calls continue uninterrupted. Overage is billed per minute at your plan rate.
Can this book appointments automatically?
Yes. The AI follows your booking rules and appointment types (and can route to the right provider where applicable).
Is there a contract?
No lock-in contracts. Cancel anytime.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. Many businesses start on Starter and upgrade as call volume increases.
Does this replace my staff?
No. It handles calls so your team can focus on customers and in-person work.
